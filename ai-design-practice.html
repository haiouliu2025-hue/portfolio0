01 / Overview

Expanding an established design practice with AI.

This collection explores how AI can enhance my existing skills across brand IP, lettering, campaigns, print applications, digital painting and commercial imagery. The focus is on the visual outcomes: how a character becomes a wider world, an illustration becomes a brand system, or a product image becomes a campaign scene.

Creative direction, selection, composition and refinement connect these explorations into work with a clear visual purpose.

02 / Brand IP

From character identity to an expressive visual world.

The IP studies connect character, colour, typography and supporting graphics. The second image extends the character into atmospheric campaign scenes, exploring how a recognisable identity can carry a richer sense of story.

03 / Typography

Explore distinctive letterforms and brand personality.

These lettering studies explore how silhouette, rhythm, contrast and decorative detail can give a name its own personality. AI-assisted exploration broadens the visual directions available for selection and refinement.

04 / Brand applications

Turn illustration into a connected campaign and print system.

The first set presents an illustrated brand direction across packaging and supporting materials. The second extends that language into campaign layouts and outdoor applications, showing how the same visual assets can work across formats.

05 / Digital painting

Build a visual series through illustration, colour and movement.

The MOMENT series explores expressive characters and dynamic composition across three colour treatments. A consistent title and layout hold the series together while each image develops a different mood.

06 / Texture-led posters

Add depth through typography, photography and surface treatment.

The sports poster combines oversized lettering, vivid colour, photographic cut-outs and a textured surface. The result explores how material character and layered composition can make a campaign image more tactile and energetic.

07 / Commercial product imagery

From a product photograph to a campaign-ready visual direction.

This sequence places the source product image beside two atmospheric interpretations. Floral elements, lighting, colour and environment create a stronger commercial mood while keeping the product at the centre of the composition.

The display highlights the visual progression from a simple product image to a developed advertising scene.

08 / Product imagery & detail-page design

Carry the product-image direction into a complete detail page.

This study connects AI-assisted product-image optimisation with the final product detail-page design. The image collection establishes a consistent atmosphere through lighting, colour, setting and product presentation.

The detail page carries that visual direction into a structured presentation of the brand story, product features, set contents and specifications. Image treatment, typography and layout connect the emotional appeal of the product with the information needed to understand it.

09 / Creative practice

More possibilities, guided by design judgement.

Across the collection, AI expands the range of visual exploration. The design task remains to choose the right direction, build hierarchy, maintain consistency and refine the details for the intended application.

Selected AI-assisted visual explorations supplied by the project owner. These images demonstrate design directions and visual outcomes; no production specifications or campaign performance results are asserted.
