Keeping human judgement visible in AI-enabled design.

03 — Research

2025–2026

Human-in-the-loop
Research premise

AI changes production. It does not remove the need for strategic thinking.

This ongoing dissertation explores how AI is reshaping visual-design practice, and how teams can preserve human judgement, problem-solving and performance focus as increasingly automated workflows become normal.

Question

What should remain human-owned?

Rather than treating AI as a replacement for designers, the project asks where strategic judgement creates the most value: framing a problem, choosing the right evidence, setting a direction, evaluating outcomes and building learning back into the system.

Method

Listen to the people changing the work.

11Industry leaders & CEOs
95Design practitioners

The research combines interviews and survey responses to understand individual differences in AI adoption and the capabilities that matter as workflows change.

Working framework

A human-in-the-loop visual system.

01

Frame

People define the business question, audience and success criteria before tools generate an answer.

02

Generate & test

AI extends exploration, while humans retain responsibility for selection, brand fit and evidence-based evaluation.

03

Learn & govern

Performance, judgement and responsible-use principles feed back into reusable visual and operating guidelines.
