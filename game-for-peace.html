Turning a gaming collaboration into a New Year sales experience.

RoleDesign Project Management / Creative Integration
ScopeIP integration, campaign KV, gift box, e-commerce, digital & offline media
03 — Case study

2022–2023

Integrated IP campaign
Overview

Make the collaboration commercially meaningful.

Wang Xiaolu partnered with Tencent’s Game for Peace on a three-month campaign spanning Double 11, Double 12 and Chinese New Year. With RMB 6 million in total investment, the collaboration was built to drive sales through in-game exposure, purchase entry points, social media, advertising and seasonal product launches.

I worked across design project management and execution, with direct ownership of brand and e-commerce design including collaborative products, the New Year gift box, character integration and campaign rollout.

Challenge

Build one commercial system across two very different worlds.

The challenge was to make a tactical gaming universe relevant to Chinese New Year snacking while keeping Wang Xiaolu recognisable. More than 30 deliverables had to work consistently across game media, e-commerce, social, outdoor advertising, packaging and offline activation.

Audience insight

Gaming and snacking already shared the same consumption moment.

Game for Peace reached a predominantly post-90s and younger audience, closely matching Wang Xiaolu’s core consumers. Gaming and snacking also naturally coexist as leisure behaviours, while Chinese New Year added a strong seasonal need for gifting and household stock-up.

Strategic decisions

Three decisions shaped the collaboration.

01

Turn the IP into a shared character world

I integrated recognisable game codes — including the shirt, tie, work trousers, Level 3 helmet and parachute — directly into Wang Xiaolu’s character while preserving its established silhouette.

02

Design the product before the campaign

Because collaborative products required a long production lead time, I prioritised character definition, gift-box design and product styling before the wider campaign system, giving all stakeholders a clear visual foundation.

03

Connect every touchpoint back to purchase

I coordinated the brand and commerce touchpoints across internal teams and external agencies so the campaign moved coherently from in-game exposure and advertising to product pages and purchase.

Selected execution

One IP system across game, product, media and commerce.

Tencent collaboration

Connect in-game paid media with a physical New Year activation.

The paid-media placement was embedded directly inside Game for Peace, with an in-game entry point connected to the collaboration and special benefits that players could claim within the game experience.

The launch also extended into an offline activation on New Year’s Day. A campaign-wrapped bus was planned to travel from Beijing’s Guomao area to Tencent’s office, turning the media placement into a moving physical touchpoint.

Collaborative products were used as a resource exchange to engage Tencent employees, while supporting campaign materials were created for distribution across other platforms to amplify the activation.

Impact

Commercial performance validated the collaboration.

33%Product-page click-through rate
3.4%Guiding conversion rate
<60%Product-page bounce rate
200/200New Year gift boxes sold out

The collaborative products generated more than 1,000 orders during the Chinese New Year campaign period, while all 200 limited-edition gift boxes sold through.

Reflection

Creative scale requires operational scale.

The project exposed gaps in the launch workflow when only three days were initially allocated for photography, quality checks, detail-page production, testing and listing. The experience reinforced the need to map dependencies early and formalise SOPs across product, photography, content, design, review and launch.

IP collaborations need a flexible character system. Brand flexibility still needs fixed identity rules.
