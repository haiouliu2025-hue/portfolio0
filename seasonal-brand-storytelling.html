Wang Xiaolu · Lifestyle content

Turning the farming calendar into an always-on brand story.

Category Content distribution
Platform Lifestyle & social channels
Theme Food, culture & the 24 solar terms
01 — Content category
Always-on brand activity

Overview

Keep the brand present between major campaigns.

Alongside promotional activity, Wang Xiaolu uses lifestyle platforms to tell its brand story during everyday moments. The goal is to build recognition and affinity continuously, not only when a campaign is asking audiences to buy.

Content idea

Connect food with the rhythm of the seasons.

The content system links the brand closely to China’s traditional farming calendar. Each story pairs a food occasion with one of the 24 solar terms, turning seasonal knowledge into a vivid and approachable brand narrative.

01

Start with a familiar seasonal cue

Use weather, ingredients and rituals as an immediate cultural entry point.

02

Make the product part of the story

Connect the food naturally to the moment rather than inserting a separate sales message.

03

Build one recognisable series

Repeat a clear framing system so individual posts accumulate into a branded world.

Editorial system

A calendar gives the brand a natural publishing rhythm.

Solar terms provide recurring moments for storytelling across the year. Illustration, seasonal colour, food pairing and concise cultural copy work together as a flexible template—consistent enough to feel owned, and varied enough to remain fresh.

秋分Autumn Equinox
霜降Frost’s Descent
寒露Cold Dew
白露White Dew

Brand value

A brand atmosphere with a story behind it.

By weaving product, food culture and the farming calendar together, the series creates a richer lifestyle context for Wang Xiaolu. Everyday publishing becomes a long-term narrative asset: timely, recognisable and grounded in cultural memory.

Food becomes the bridge between a seasonal moment and the brand.
